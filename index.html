Understanding U.S. Insurance Risk & Climate Data

Why Insurance Risk Matters

The cost of home insurance is no longer just about the value of the house; it is increasingly tied to the physical risks of climate change. From wildfires in the West to hurricanes in the Gulf, insurers are re-evaluating risk. Our composite score blends Climate Safety (FEMA hazard data) with Insurance Affordability (premium burden relative to income) to give you a clear picture of where a home is truly safe and affordable to insure.

How Our 6-Axis Score is Calculated

We do not rely on gut feelings. Our rankings are built on a data-driven 6-axis model:

  • Climate Safety: Derived from the FEMA National Risk Index, factoring in expected annual loss from natural disasters.
  • Insurance Affordability: The percentage of median household income required to pay average premiums.
  • Housing Affordability: The ratio of median home prices to median household incomes.
  • Community Resilience & Social Resilience: Measures of a community's ability to bounce back after a disaster (infrastructure, social cohesion).
  • Housing Stability: Indicators of market volatility and foreclosure risk.

How to Use This Data

Use the Customize Weights sliders above to match your priorities. If you are a retiree looking for climate safety, slide that weight up to 100%. If you are a first-time homebuyer on a tight budget, increase the Insurance and Housing Affordability weights. The rankings and the Top 100 table will update in real-time, allowing you to find the perfect county that fits your risk tolerance and financial goals.
