🌊 Flood Insurance Gap

Standard HO-3 policies never cover flood damage — even when water enters through a broken window.

⚠️ The Coverage Gap

Every standard HO-3 policy explicitly excludes flood damage. This applies even when water enters through a broken window, a failed sump pump, or storm surge. 25% of all flood claims come from outside FEMA high-risk zones.

📊 Key Statistics

Average flood claim (severe event) $100,000+
Flood claims outside high-risk zones ~25%
NFIP waiting period after purchase 30 days
Max NFIP structure coverage $250,000

✅ Do You Need Additional Coverage?

Check each item that applies to your home:

  • ☐ My property is in or near a FEMA flood zone (A, AE, V, VE)
  • ☐ My home has a basement or crawlspace
  • ☐ I live within 1 mile of a river, lake, or coast
  • ☐ My mortgage lender requires flood insurance
  • ☐ Recent development has increased runoff in my area

If you checked 2 or more, contact your insurance agent about supplemental coverage.

🛡️ Coverage Options

NFIP (National Flood Insurance Program)

Federally backed. Max $250,000 for structure, $100,000 for contents. Available in participating communities. 30-day waiting period.

Private Flood Insurance

Higher limits, faster claims, sometimes lower premiums in low-moderate risk zones. Offered by Lloyd's syndicates, Neptune, and others.

Excess Flood Policy

Stacks on top of NFIP for homes above $250,000. Covers gap between NFIP limit and rebuilding cost.
