🌪️ Tornado & Hail Insurance Gap

Wind from tornadoes is covered — but high percentage deductibles for hail and cosmetic damage exclusions create real gaps.

⚠️ The Coverage Gap

Tornado wind is covered by standard HO-3. The gap is in the deductible structure: many policies in Tornado Alley states apply percentage deductibles (1-5% of dwelling) specifically to wind/hail claims. Additionally, cosmetic damage exclusions (metal roof denting, siding dings) are increasingly common — damage that looks bad but doesn't cause structural loss.

📊 Key Statistics

🛡️ Coverage Options

Standard HO-3 (wind covered)

Tornado and hail wind damage are covered by default. The gap is in your deductible structure.

Cosmetic Damage Endorsement

Restores coverage for cosmetic-only damage (roof dents, siding marks) that modern policies often exclude.

Extended Replacement Cost

Covers rebuild cost above policy limit (often 125-150%), critical in tornado zones where rebuild costs spike post-disaster.
