💡 Why This Matters

Most homeowners assume their insurance covers "everything." But standard HO-3 policies exclude flood, earthquake, and landslide entirely, and treat hurricane wind and tornado damage with percentage deductibles that can reach 5% of your home's value.

When a disaster strikes, homeowners discover the gap after the fact. This guide shows you where the risks are highest and what coverage actually protects you.
