🌀 Hurricane Wind Insurance Gap

Wind damage from named storms carries a separate percentage deductible — often 2-5% of your home's value.

⚠️ The Coverage Gap

Hurricane wind is covered by standard policies, but with a critical caveat: most coastal policies apply a percentage deductible (2%, 3%, or 5% of dwelling coverage) for named storms. On a $500K home with a 5% deductible, you pay $25,000 before insurance kicks in. In some FL coastal zones, homeowners must buy separate wind policies entirely.

📊 Key Statistics

Typical named-storm deductible 2-5% of dwelling
$500K home, 5% deductible $25,000 out of pocket
Separate wind policies in coastal FL Common
Wind vs. water causation disputes Frequent

🛡️ Coverage Options

Standard HO-3 with % hurricane deductible

Covers wind damage but with a percentage deductible for named storms. Read your declarations page to confirm the percentage.

Separate Wind-Only Policy

Required in some coastal FL zones. Offered by Citizens Property Insurance (FL state insurer) or surplus lines.

Citizens Property Insurance (FL)

Florida's state-backed insurer of last resort. Coverage caps apply; requires proof of no private market availability.
