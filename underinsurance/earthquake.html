🏚️ Earthquake Insurance Gap

Standard policies exclude all earth movement — earthquakes, aftershocks, and related damage require a separate policy.

⚠️ The Coverage Gap

Standard HO-3 policies exclude 'earth movement' — a category covering earthquakes, landslides, sinkholes, and mudflows. California has the highest concentration of risk, but the New Madrid Seismic Zone (MO, TN, AR) and South Carolina also face significant exposure.

📊 Key Statistics

U.S. homes at high earthquake risk ~14 million
Earthquake insurance penetration (CA) ~13%
Typical earthquake deductible 10-15% of home value
CEA max residential coverage $1M (structure)

🛡️ Coverage Options

CEA (California Earthquake Authority)

State-backed insurer for CA residents. Coverage up to $1M structure + $100K contents. Sold through participating private insurers.

Stand-alone Earthquake Policy

Offered by private carriers (GeoVera, Palomar, etc.). Available nationwide. Deductibles 5-25% of dwelling coverage.

Difference in Conditions (DIC)

Broadens HO-3 to include earthquake, flood, and other exclusions. Common for high-value homes.
