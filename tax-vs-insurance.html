Insurance vs. Property Tax

In many U.S. counties, rising insurance premiums now exceed property taxes. Explore the reversal.

💡 The Hidden Cost Reversal

Traditionally, property taxes were the largest recurring cost of homeownership. However, climate risks have driven insurance premiums so high that in many counties (especially in Louisiana), insurance now costs 15-20x more than property taxes.

📉 Impact on Population (Top 100 Counties)

Among the Top 100 counties where insurance exceeds property taxes, 81 out of 100 have experienced a population decline over the past 5 years. This suggests that skyrocketing insurance costs may be a significant driver of out-migration in these high-risk areas.
