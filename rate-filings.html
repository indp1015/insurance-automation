💡 Why Rate Filings Matter

Unlike most consumer products, home insurance rates must be filed with state regulators before they change. This creates an unusual transparency window: you can see what a carrier requested weeks or months before your renewal arrives.

For independent analysis of national rate trends, see BoringRate's rate filings tracker — an independent research site that parses SERFF-approved filings across states.

What this tracker doesn't cover: Only Texas filings are shown here. Other states (CA, FL, LA, NY) publish filings through their own regulators or SERFF.
